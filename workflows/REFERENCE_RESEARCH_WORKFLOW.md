# Reference Research Workflow

## Sources
Use user-provided screenshots, URLs, brand guidelines, existing product screens, and agent-led research. Prefer primary sources for library APIs and platform guidance.

## Analyze references
For each useful reference, identify:
- purpose and audience;
- information hierarchy;
- layout and composition;
- typography and color;
- image treatment;
- navigation and interactions;
- motion behaviour;
- responsive patterns;
- strengths, weaknesses, and suitability.

## Synthesize
Compare references and extract transferable principles. Build an original direction suited to the user's product. Do not copy brand assets, text, proprietary illustrations, or a distinctive design wholesale.

## Research quality
- Prefer current, credible sources.
- Verify library APIs in official documentation.
- Distinguish observed facts from interpretation.
- Keep research proportional to task complexity.
- Do not claim to have visited or inspected sources that were not accessed.
- Record important URLs and review dates when they influence a major decision.

## Deliverable
For substantial work, provide a concise reference synthesis: what was learned, what principles were adopted, what was deliberately not copied, and how the result fits the product.
